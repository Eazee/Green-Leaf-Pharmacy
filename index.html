Fun Facts About Drug Interactions

Small details that make a big difference to how your medicine works.

Grapefruit & statins

Grapefruit juice can block the enzyme that breaks down many statins, causing them to build up in your body to unsafe levels.

Dairy & antibiotics

Calcium in milk can bind to certain antibiotics like tetracycline, making them harder for your body to absorb.

Leafy greens & warfarin

Vitamin K-rich greens like spinach can reduce the effect of blood thinners. Consistency matters more than avoidance.

Coffee & asthma meds

Caffeine can amplify the stimulant effects of some asthma medications, leading to jitteriness and a faster heart rate.

Taking meds with food

Some drugs need food to absorb properly, while others work best on an empty stomach — timing changes everything.

Alcohol & painkillers

Mixing alcohol with acetaminophen or NSAIDs raises the risk of liver and stomach damage — a common, avoidable danger.
